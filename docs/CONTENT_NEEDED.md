# Content Needed Before Public Launch

The site intentionally avoids filling gaps with invented claims. Supply and approve the following before treating it as a public booking destination.

## Contact and booking delivery

- Booking inquiry email address.
- Phone number, if it should be public.
- Approved social profile labels and URLs.
- A secure owner-approved form endpoint, expected fields, receiving workflow, and confirmation/cancellation behavior.
- Privacy-policy details for the form: what is collected, why, who receives it, retention period, and contact/rights route.

## Business facts and offerings

- Owner-approved biography, preferred public wording, and current location/service-area detail.
- Current offerings, event types, travel boundaries, and any constraints that should be public.
- Approved pricing/packages, inclusions, availability policy, insurance information, equipment details, and venue relationships only if Phelix wants to state them.
- Any approved testimonials, client/venue permissions, awards, certifications, or press links. None are represented until supplied and approved.

## Media

- Approved professional photo, logo/wordmark, video, and mix files or final public URLs.
- Captions, credit/usage rights, accessibility alt text, and confirmation that identifiable people/venues may be shown.
- Final social, streaming, and media-platform destinations.
- Confirm that each Spotify playlist intended for the website is public and embeddable.
- Review the provisional playlist descriptions and category tags; the starter metadata is based on playlist titles because the connected inventory did not expose track-level contents.
- Approve final playlist titles, descriptions, category tags, featured order, and whether any third-party saved playlists may be shown.
- Supply additional owned playlist links for era-specific and event-moment collections as the library grows.

## Legal and launch review

- A reviewed privacy policy to replace the current placeholder.
- Business/legal identity and any required contact/address disclosures.
- Review of form submission, HTTPS/custom-domain behavior, mobile layout, canonical URL, and all visible copy after the real contact route is configured.
